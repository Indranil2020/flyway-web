How we work

Four things we won't compromise on.

Small groups, hard caps

Four to eight guests. We do not merge parties to fill seats, and high-altitude trips run smaller still.

One guide, whole trip

The person who meets you at the airport is the person birding with you on the last morning. No handoffs.

Every checklist filed

Complete eBird checklists on every trip, under the trip name. Your sightings become public data.

Local money stays local

Homestays, village kitchens and local drivers wherever the standard allows it. Guiding fees are paid direct.
